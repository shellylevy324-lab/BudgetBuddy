# Community Signs — first classroom pilot

## Trial flow
1. Select a student and enable Community Signs in Teacher Center. Assign a sign set, response level, number of trials, audio, and distractor-removal interval.
2. Launch Community Signs from the student Training Station. Each trial randomly positions the correct answer among three choices.
3. When audio is enabled, the instruction and each choice are spoken before answers unlock. Audio depends on the browser speech synthesis voices and device volume.
4. Response latency begins when choices unlock. Replay restarts the presentation and latency clock; it is not recorded separately.
5. In least-to-most mode, one incorrect option disappears after the configured wait time and another after the configured step interval. Wrong answers end the trial and are counted as incorrect.
6. The completion screen displays accuracy, independent correct, average latency, prompt-level counts and per-trial results. Session Engine receives the original detailed records.

## Classroom verification
- Test with audio enabled and disabled, baseline and least-to-most, and 5/10/15/20 trials.
- Verify student assignment, reinforcement, session history, and cloud persistence on your school devices before using records for decisions.
- Check sign images and spoken directions for each assigned set; confirm browser audio is enabled.
- The module is a supervised teaching tool, not a substitute for real-world safety instruction.

## Scope
Existing Budget Buddy files and other modules are retained. The pilot changes are limited to Community Signs JS/HTML/CSS and this guide.
