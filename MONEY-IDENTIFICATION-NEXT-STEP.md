# Money Identification — Next Skill Target

Money Identification is registered as the next Buddy Skills activity but is intentionally not shown to students in v3.0.0.

## Proposed instructional progression

1. **Level 1 — Match identical money**
   - penny to penny, $1 bill to $1 bill
   - one target and two visually distinct distractors
2. **Level 2 — Identify by name**
   - penny, nickel, dime, quarter, one-dollar bill, five-dollar bill, ten-dollar bill, twenty-dollar bill
3. **Level 3 — Identify by value**
   - 1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢, $1, $5, $10, $20
4. **Level 4 — Generalize across examples**
   - front/back of coins and bills, different orientations, photographs in functional contexts
